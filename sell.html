The buy list

What we’re buying right now

Square D, Siemens, Eaton / Cutler-Hammer, ABB, GE, Westinghouse and every other major brand. Quantity helps, but singles of the right item get paid too.

Breakers & Distribution

  • Molded case circuit breakers (incl. Square D PowerPact)
  • Miniature & industrial-frame breakers
  • Panelboards & distribution panels
  • Switchboards & switchgear
  • Meter sockets

Busway & Power

  • Bus duct & busway runs
  • Bus plugs & fittings
  • Transformers — dry-type & padmount
  • MCC lineups & buckets

Controls & Motors

  • Starters & contactors
  • Variable frequency drives (VFDs)
  • Single-phase motors under 20 HP
  • Lighting & ballasts

Wire & Raceway

  • Wire & cable, all gauges
  • Conduit & fittings
  • Wire troughing
  • Job-site overstock & returns

A few things we pass on

So nobody wastes a trip — we currently do not buy:

  • Three-phase motors 20 HP and up
  • UPS systems
  • Transfer switches
  • PDU units
  • Disconnects
  • Fused pullouts
Get your number

Tell us what you’re sitting on.

The fastest route is still a text to 404-831-3600 with pictures — but the form works too, and we answer both the same day.

  • Include brand names and amp ratings if you can see them.
  • Rough quantity is fine — “about three pallets” tells us plenty.
  • Tell us where it is; we’ll figure out the trucking.
